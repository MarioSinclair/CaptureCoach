What this tool does. It judges photograph quality and view coverage, nothing else. It does not read, transcribe or verify label text, serial numbers, device identity or ownership, and it makes no claim about data sanitisation or regulatory compliance. Those belong to the documented ITAD process this capture step feeds, not to a camera assistant.

Where the AI is. Blur, exposure, glare and whole-device framing are measured in OpenCV against thresholds calibrated on the supplied photographs. A vision model is asked only two things it cannot measure: which view a photo actually shows, and whether anything is covering the DEMO label. No model output can override a measurement.
